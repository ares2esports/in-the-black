Welcome to In the Black

1. Open the app on payday
Every 15th and end of month, the Home tab shows exactly which cards to pay and how much. No math needed.
2. Pay your cards, then tap Apply
Make the payments at your banks, then hit the Apply button here. Balances update and the plan recalculates.
3. Watch your progress grow
Cards get a green PAID badge as they hit zero. The big number shrinks, the green bar grows. Small wins stack up fast.
One thing to do first
In Setup, tap Edit on each card and enter your real APR, due date, and credit limit from your statements. The estimates get much sharper.
